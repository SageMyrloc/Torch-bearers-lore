# Aelthir Vaelorn

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Aelthir Vaelorn was the wizard who used the [[The Gloamheart Prism|Gloamheart Prism]] to create the demiplane known as [[The Veil of Aeluran]].

## Known Facts

- Aelthir Vaelorn was a wizard.
- He created the Veil of Aeluran.
- He used the Gloamheart Prism as the power source for that creation.
- His name, achievement and use of the Prism are known to the expedition before it enters the demiplane.

## Unknowns

- Why he created the Veil.
- How he used the Prism to create it.
- What became of him afterward.
- What connection, if any, he has to the Veil's present condition.

## Connections

- [[The Veil of Aeluran]] — The demiplane he created.
- [[The Gloamheart Prism]] — The artifact that powered its creation.
