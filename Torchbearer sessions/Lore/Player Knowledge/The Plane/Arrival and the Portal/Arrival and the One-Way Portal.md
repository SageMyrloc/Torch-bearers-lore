# Arrival and the One-Way Portal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Torchbearers entered the demiplane through a portal that permits arrival but offers no known passage back.

## Known Facts

- The portal was understood to be one-way before the expedition crossed it.
- Arrivals emerge in the town's central marketplace.
- The first recorded arrival caused [[The Central Lantern]] to relight.
- Later arrivals have coincided with the lantern's light becoming stronger.
- No cause for the lantern's response has been established.

## Unknowns

- Why the portal leads to this particular place.
- Why the portal is one-way.
- Whether another route out of the demiplane exists.
- Why living arrivals affect the central lantern.

## Connections

- [[The Pathfinder Society Expedition]] — The group entering through the portal.
- [[The Central Lantern]] — The structure that responded to their arrival.
- [[The Protected Marketplace]] — The secure area surrounding the point of arrival.
