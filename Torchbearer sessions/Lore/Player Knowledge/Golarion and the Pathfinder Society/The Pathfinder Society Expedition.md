# The Pathfinder Society Expedition

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The player characters are members of the Pathfinder Society who voluntarily entered the portal to explore, discover and recover wealth.

## Known Facts

- The expedition is connected to the Pathfinder Society.
- Its members crossed the portal knowingly despite its one-way nature.
- Exploration, discovery and the recovery of wealth are recognised reasons for joining the expedition.
- Individual characters may have additional personal, religious or scholarly motives.

## Current Position

The expedition's secure foothold is limited to [[The Protected Marketplace]] and the buildings that face into it. Further exploration requires confronting [[The Mist]].

- [[Arrival and the One-Way Portal]] — The expedition's point of entry.
- [[The Protected Marketplace]] — Its present base of operations.
