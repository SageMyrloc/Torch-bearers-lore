# The Glyph Network

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The glyphs are components of an old network that carries the central lantern's protective magic through the town. They transmit power; they are not themselves its source.

## Established Truths

- The glyphs once allowed the lantern's protection to extend across the town.
- The network is presently inactive, damaged or otherwise unable to carry that protection.
- Finding and activating glyphs is the structural objective of the first twelve sessions.
- Each successful activation allows the lantern's protection to spread farther and makes part of the town safe again.
- Players begin with no knowledge of the glyphs or their purpose.

## Unresolved Decisions

- The precise number of glyphs and whether every session activates exactly one.
- Whether glyphs must be activated in a fixed order.
- What powers, blocks, damages or corrupts individual nodes.
- Whether expansion is permanent, reversible or vulnerable to sabotage.
- How quickly the network spreads light after activation.
- Whether the network has functions beyond repelling the mist.

## Use in Play

- Introduce the existence and purpose of the network through discoveries in the early sessions.
- Provide more than one route to each essential revelation.
- Let each activation create a visible, persistent change to the settlement.
- Do not place a player-facing Glyphs article in the vault until the party has discovered the subject.
