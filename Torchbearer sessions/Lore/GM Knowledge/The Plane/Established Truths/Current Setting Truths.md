# Current Setting Truths

> [!abstract] At a glance
> This register contains the setting facts currently confirmed for the opening of the Torchbearers campaign. It intentionally preserves the deeper mysteries behind those facts.

## Established Truths

- The campaign uses Pathfinder Second Edition and begins with characters from Golarion.
- The characters are members of the Pathfinder Society.
- They voluntarily entered a one-way portal into a mist-filled demiplane to explore, discover and recover wealth.
- The demiplane is named the **Veil of Aeluran**.
- It was created by the wizard **Aelthir Vaelorn**.
- Aelthir was an obscure elven wizard active during the **Age of Darkness**, more than four thousand years ago.
- His name survived mainly within specialist scholarship concerning the creation of permanent artificial demiplanes.
- His surviving research is known through scattered quotations, copied passages and later references collectively called the **Vaelorn Fragments**.
- Aelthir used the **Gloamheart Prism** as the power source for the Veil's creation.
- A recurring theme in material attributed to Aelthir is shelter, isolation and preservation in the aftermath of Earthfall.
- The Veil was created as an act of preservation: a refuge intended to endure beyond disasters affecting Golarion.
- The precise details of Aelthir's motives, the people originally intended to live there and the method of creation remain unresolved.
- Supernatural mist covers the demiplane and is exceptionally dangerous without lantern protection.
- Creatures live within the mist and can attack exposed travellers.
- A giant lantern in the central marketplace relit when the first person arrived.
- Its light has strengthened as more people have arrived, but currently protects only the marketplace.
- Personal lanterns are lit from the central lantern and provide one day of active use.
- Closing a personal lantern's shutters prevents it emitting magic and preserves its remaining duration.
- A network of glyphs once carried the central lantern's protection across the town.
- The opening twelve sessions concern finding and activating those glyphs to reclaim the town.
- The current principal antagonist is provisionally a worm-that-walks wizard formed from the reanimated corpse of Aelthir Vaelorn.
- The antagonist's nature and identity are not known to the players and should be revealed much later.

## Knowledge Boundaries

- Players know the Veil's name, that Aelthir created it, and that he used the Gloamheart Prism as its power source.
- Players are briefed that Aelthir was an obscure Age-of-Darkness planar researcher whose work survives only in fragments.
- Players may know that some modern scholars connect his work with preservation after Earthfall, but they do not begin with certainty about his exact motive.
- Players begin with no knowledge of the glyphs.
- Players possess only minimal reliable knowledge about the mist.
- Players have no starting knowledge of the principal antagonist or Aelthir's fate.
- Ordinary Golarion knowledge remains available when appropriate to character background and PF2E Recall Knowledge.

## Deliberately Unresolved

- The exact circumstances that drove Aelthir to create the Veil and how he populated or intended to populate it.
- The precise method by which he created the Veil.
- The origin and true nature of the Gloamheart Prism.
- The meaning of **Aeluran**.
- The origin, purpose and true nature of the mist.
- The reason the portal is one-way.
- The reason arrivals strengthen the central lantern.
- The full history of the town and its protective network.
- The exact relationship between Aelthir, the reanimated wizard and the mind or soul within that creature.
