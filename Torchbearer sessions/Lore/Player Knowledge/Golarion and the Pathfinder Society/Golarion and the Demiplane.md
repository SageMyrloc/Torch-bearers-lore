# Golarion and the Demiplane

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Torchbearers come from Golarion, but the campaign begins after they cross into a separate demiplane called [[The Veil of Aeluran]].

## Known Facts

- Golarion remains the characters' home world and the source of their cultures, faiths, magic and prior experience.
- The expedition's destination is the Veil of Aeluran, a demiplane rather than an unexplored region of Golarion.
- The Veil was created by the wizard [[Aelthir Vaelorn]].
- Aelthir used [[The Gloamheart Prism]] as the power source for its creation.
- Crossing the portal does not remove or rewrite ordinary Pathfinder Second Edition setting knowledge.
- The demiplane may contain phenomena for which Golarion offers no reliable explanation.
