# Creative Additions Summary - Initial Knowledge Articles

## Purpose

This file records wording, structure and working assumptions introduced while creating the initial player- and GM-knowledge articles. Nothing under **Provisional Additions** becomes campaign canon until approved by the GM.

## Confirmed Facts Supplied by the GM

- The demiplane is named **the Veil of Aeluran**.
- Its creator is the wizard **Aelthir Vaelorn**.
- The **Gloamheart Prism** was the power source used to create the Veil.
- Players begin knowing these three basic facts, but not the deeper history behind them.
- The glyphs transmit the central lantern's protection rather than generating it.
- The principal antagonist is provisionally a worm-that-walks wizard formed from the reanimated corpse of the wizard, with the truth reserved for later in the campaign.

## Earlier Provisional Additions and Interpretations

1. **Golarion knowledge boundary:** Characters are assumed to know broad public information appropriate to their background, while specialist details are handled through relevant PF2E knowledge and Lore. This is an authoring policy, not new world lore.
2. **Demiplane knowledge overrides analogy:** Familiarity with a similar Golarion phenomenon may suggest an approach but does not automatically reveal how the demiplane works. This is an authoring policy.
3. **Individual character motives:** The Pathfinder Society article allows personal, religious or scholarly motives in addition to exploration and wealth. These are possibilities for player characters, not established motives for any named character.
4. **Unverified creature descriptions:** The creature article treats early descriptions from poor visibility as potentially unreliable. This supports the GM's instruction that knowledge of the mist is minimal; no specific false report has been invented.
5. **No player Glyphs article at campaign start:** This is an editorial safeguard. The existence of a file, title or backlink would reveal forbidden knowledge.
6. **The Reanimated Wizard is marked draft:** The antagonist concept is recorded because the GM said it is the current plan “for now.” Its undeveloped details remain unresolved.

## New Provisional Additions: Veil, Aelthir and Prism

1. **Aelthir as the reanimated wizard:** The new GM articles interpret “the wizard” whose corpse is reanimated as Aelthir Vaelorn. This is the most direct connection between the supplied facts but should be corrected if the GM intended two different wizards.
2. **Ethereal construction:** The Veil is provisionally described as a folded and stabilised region of the Ethereal Plane rather than matter created from nothing.
3. **Prism as anchor and engine:** The Gloamheart Prism is provisionally an ongoing planar anchor and energy-conversion engine, not a battery exhausted during creation.
4. **One magical system:** The central lantern provisionally filters the Prism's power into a safe form; glyphs distribute it and personal lanterns store a limited charge.
5. **Dual effect of glyph restoration:** Activating glyphs genuinely makes the town safer but may also wake dormant systems, carry signals deeper into the Veil, and expand the reanimated wizard's awareness.
6. **Aelthir's background:** He is provisionally an elven planar architect using conjuration, abjuration and fey-influenced geometry.
7. **Aelthir's motive:** He is provisionally framed as creating a refuge for preservation rather than seeking conquest or personal dominion.
8. **The Prism's influence:** The artifact provisionally exerts subtle pressure toward obsession, certainty and control instead of openly speaking or possessing its wielder.
9. **The Prism's appearance:** Its black-violet facets, impossible refracted colours and heart-like central inclusion are newly proposed visual details.
10. **The antagonist's fragmented memory:** The reanimated wizard provisionally retains pieces of Aelthir's technical memory and senses restored glyphs as parts of a severed magical nervous system.
11. **Destruction has consequences:** Removing or destroying the Prism may threaten the Veil, allowing cleansing, retuning or replacing its functions to become possible alternatives.
12. **Meaning of Aeluran:** No meaning or identity has been assigned to Aeluran. This is deliberately preserved as a future mystery.

## Unresolved Rules and Lore Preserved

- Whether a fully shuttered personal lantern provides any protection.
- Whether partial shuttering is possible.
- How personal lantern duration is measured in play.
- Whether every one of the twelve sessions corresponds to exactly one glyph.
- Whether glyphs must be activated in a fixed order.
- Why Aelthir created the Veil and what Aeluran means.
- Who created the Gloamheart Prism and how Aelthir obtained it.
- Whether the mist is native to the Veil, a by-product, or an invading force.
- Whether Aelthir's soul survives within the reanimated creature.
- The Prism's current location and whether the Veil can survive without it.
