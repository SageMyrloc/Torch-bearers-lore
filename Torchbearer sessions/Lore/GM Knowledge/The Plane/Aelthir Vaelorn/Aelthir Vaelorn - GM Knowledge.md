# Aelthir Vaelorn - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Aelthir Vaelorn was an obscure elven planar researcher of the Age of Darkness who created the Veil using the Gloamheart Prism. His work grew from a desire to preserve life and civilisation from catastrophes like Earthfall; his later fate remains hidden from the players.

## Confirmed Campaign Canon

- Aelthir Vaelorn was an elf and a wizard.
- He lived more than four thousand years ago during the Age of Darkness.
- He was obscure even historically, known mainly to specialists studying permanent artificial demiplanes.
- Most of his original work was lost. Modern knowledge comes through scattered quotations, copied passages and later references collectively called the **Vaelorn Fragments**.
- His research focused on planar permanence: creating a demiplane able to survive independently of its creator.
- His work was shaped by the aftermath of Earthfall and by the problem of preserving people, knowledge and civilisation from catastrophes affecting Golarion.
- He created the Veil of Aeluran as a refuge intended to endure beyond such disasters.
- He used the Gloamheart Prism as the creation's power source.
- Players receive a simplified historical version of this information before entering the Veil.
- His later history and fate are not known to the players at campaign start.

## Working Interpretation

- Aelthir is the wizard whose corpse later became the campaign's worm-that-walks antagonist.
- The resulting creature is Aelthir's reanimated body, but it is not yet decided whether it is truly Aelthir.

## Provisional Character Foundation

> [!warning] Not yet approved canon

- Aelthir's magic combined conjuration, abjuration and geometry influenced by fey traditions.
- The Prism did not openly command him. It encouraged certainty, control and the belief that any sacrifice could be justified if the Veil survived.
- When Aelthir recognised that the system was changing him or the realm, he attempted to contain the damage rather than abandon his creation.

## Portraying His Legacy

- Early records should show brilliance, patience and genuine concern for the Veil's inhabitants.
- His concern with preservation should predate the Prism, so his original motive remains sincere rather than being created by corruption.
- Later records can become more absolute: safety becomes obedience, preservation becomes confinement, and every problem becomes evidence that the system needs greater control.
- His mistakes should remain understandable. He should feel tragic before he feels monstrous.
- Surviving notes may contradict one another because they were written at different stages of his life and the Prism's influence, not because every record is deliberately deceptive.

## Decisions to Preserve

- What **Aeluran** means to him.
- Whether he personally witnessed Earthfall or was born into its immediate aftermath.
- Who first entered the Veil with him and how its original population was chosen.
- Whether his soul remains within the reanimated creature.
- Whether he died stopping a failure, caused the failure, or was murdered.
- Whether he understood the Prism's true nature before using it.
