# The Veil of Aeluran - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Aelthir Vaelorn created the Veil of Aeluran using the Gloamheart Prism. The exact purpose and method remain open, but the Prism, lanterns and glyph network can form one coherent system.

## Confirmed Campaign Canon

- The demiplane is named the **Veil of Aeluran**.
- [[Aelthir Vaelorn - GM Knowledge|Aelthir Vaelorn]] created it.
- [[The Gloamheart Prism - GM Knowledge|The Gloamheart Prism]] was the power source used in its creation.
- Supernatural mist fills the realm beyond areas protected by lantern light.
- The town once used a glyph network to carry the central lantern's protection.

## Provisional Working Foundation

> [!warning] Not yet approved canon
> The following ideas are designed to connect the existing elements without settling every mystery.

- The Veil was formed by folding and stabilising a region of the Ethereal Plane rather than creating matter from nothing.
- The Gloamheart Prism remains both the plane's anchor and its engine. The Veil persists because the artifact continues to hold its boundaries in place.
- The central lantern transforms the Prism's raw power into a form safe for living inhabitants.
- The glyph network carries that filtered power through the town. Personal lanterns hold a small, temporary measure of the same protection.
- The mist is not automatically the Prism's creation. It may be the Veil's original ethereal substance, a symptom of failing stability, or something that entered later.

## Campaign Use

- Restoring glyphs genuinely makes the town safer.
- Each restored connection can also wake dormant systems, carry signals deeper into the Veil, or alert something that remembers the old network.
- This gives the opening victories an honest benefit and a gradual, unsettling consequence without making the players' work a mistake.
- Repeated imagery of refraction, divided light and many-faceted geometry can foreshadow the Prism before it becomes central to the plot.

## Questions Preserved for Later

- What **Aeluran** means and why Aelthir chose the name.
- Why Aelthir created the Veil.
- Whether the town was built in the Veil or transported into it.
- Whether the Prism's continued operation is necessary for the plane's survival.
- Whether the hostile mist is a failure of the Veil, an invading force, or an intended part of it.
- Whether reclaiming the whole town helps contain the greater threat or reconnects it to the Prism.
