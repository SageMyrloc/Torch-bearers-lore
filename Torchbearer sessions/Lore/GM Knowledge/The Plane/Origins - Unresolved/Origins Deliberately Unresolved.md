# Origins Deliberately Unresolved

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Veil's name, creator and power source are established, but their deeper history is intentionally undefined so the setting can grow through play.

## Established Foundation

- The demiplane is the Veil of Aeluran.
- Aelthir Vaelorn created it.
- The Gloamheart Prism supplied the power used in its creation.

## Questions Without Canonical Answers

- Why Aelthir created the Veil and what he intended it to become.
- What **Aeluran** means.
- Who or what created the Gloamheart Prism and how Aelthir obtained it.
- The precise method by which the Prism's power created the demiplane.
- Whether the town was built within the Veil or brought from elsewhere.
- Whether the mist is native, introduced or produced by the plane.
- Who built the lantern and glyph network, beyond its connection to the Veil's magic.
- Why the portal is one-way.
- Why arrivals strengthen the central lantern.
- What caused Aelthir's death and the reanimation of his corpse.

## Design Rule

Early clues may support several interpretations, but no document, NPC or successful check should establish one of these answers as fact until the GM approves it as campaign canon.

## Permitted Early Discoveries

- Observable rules governing a specific location or phenomenon.
- Fragments of Aelthir's work and the town's history.
- Contradictory testimony, religious interpretations and incomplete records.
- Evidence that rules out a theory without identifying the final truth.
- Connections between the Prism, lanterns, glyphs, mist and antagonist that do not explain their ultimate origin.
