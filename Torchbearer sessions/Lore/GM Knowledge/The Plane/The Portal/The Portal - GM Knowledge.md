# The Portal - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The portal is the expedition's one-way entrance from Golarion to the town's central marketplace. Its deeper function is intentionally undefined.

## Established Truths

- The characters knew the portal was one-way before entering.
- It delivers arrivals to the central marketplace.
- The first arrival caused the central lantern to relight.
- Further arrivals have strengthened that light.

## Player Knowledge

The contents of [[Arrival and the One-Way Portal]] are safe for players at campaign start.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Who or what made the portal.
- Whether its one-way nature is inherent, damaged or imposed.
- Whether a return route exists elsewhere.
- Whether the portal selects, changes or consumes anything from those who pass through it.
- Why arrivals affect the central lantern.

## Use in Play

Do not provide an authoritative explanation through early magical examination. Investigation may establish observable behaviour, rule out theories or reveal clues without deciding the portal's origin.
