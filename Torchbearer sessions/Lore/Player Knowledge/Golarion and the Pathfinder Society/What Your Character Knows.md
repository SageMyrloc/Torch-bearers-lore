# What Your Character Knows

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Torchbearers retain the ordinary knowledge they would possess on Golarion. Reliable information about the [[The Veil of Aeluran|Veil of Aeluran]] is extremely limited when the campaign begins.

## Knowledge of Golarion

A character may know the broad, public facts appropriate to their upbringing, ancestry, faith, profession, training and time with the Pathfinder Society. This can include common knowledge about:

- Golarion's peoples, nations and cultures.
- Widely worshipped gods and their public teachings.
- Well-known creatures, hazards and magical traditions.
- The public purpose and practices of the Pathfinder Society.

Specialist facts, obscure history, exact monster abilities, secret religious teachings and unfamiliar cultures may require Recall Knowledge or an appropriate Lore skill during play.

## Knowledge of the Veil

The expedition knows that its destination is a demiplane called the **Veil of Aeluran**, created by a wizard named [[Aelthir Vaelorn]] using [[The Gloamheart Prism]] as a power source. Beyond those basic facts, no body of reliable scholarship about the Veil is available to the expedition at the start of the campaign.

Characters may recognise similarities to phenomena from Golarion, but resemblance is not proof that those phenomena follow the same rules here.

The player-facing articles in this vault represent the expedition's currently reliable shared knowledge. They can expand as the Torchbearers make discoveries.
