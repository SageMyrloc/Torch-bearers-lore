# Personal Lanterns - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Personal lanterns are portable vessels for the central lantern's protective magic. Their finite duration makes exploration beyond safe light costly.

## Established Truths

- They are magical and are lit from the central lantern.
- Each provides one day of active use.
- Shuttering a lantern prevents its magic from being emitted and preserves remaining duration.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Whether a fully shuttered lantern provides any protection. This was not confirmed and must not be assumed in play.
- Whether partial shuttering is possible or changes consumption.
- How duration is measured and communicated to players.
- Whether lantern charge is individual, transferable or refillable during an expedition.
- What happens if a lantern is damaged, stolen, extinguished or taken beyond its bearer.

## Use in Play

Resolve the operating rules before lantern duration becomes a tactical subsystem. Until then, describe only the confirmed behaviour and avoid using an unanswered detail as a surprise punishment.
