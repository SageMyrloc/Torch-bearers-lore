# The Gloamheart Prism - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Gloamheart Prism supplied the power Aelthir used to create the Veil. A useful working direction is that it remains the realm's anchor, while the lantern network filters and distributes its otherwise dangerous power.

## Confirmed Campaign Canon

- The Gloamheart Prism is a magical artifact of immense power.
- Aelthir Vaelorn used it as the power source when he created the Veil of Aeluran.
- Players know these basic historical facts at campaign start.
- Its origin, current location and true properties remain unknown to them.

## Provisional Nature

> [!warning] Not yet approved canon

- The Prism is not a battery that was exhausted during creation. It is an engine that refracts planar energy into different expressions and an anchor that holds the Veil together.
- Its surface appears black or deep violet, but light passing through it divides into colours not always visible elsewhere in the spectrum. A dark inclusion at its centre resembles a slowly beating heart, giving the artifact its name.
- The central lantern filters one expression of the Prism's power into protective light. The glyphs distribute it, while personal lanterns store a measured charge.
- Raw exposure does not cause immediate, obvious possession. The Prism subtly rewards obsession, certainty and escalating attempts to control complex systems.
- Its influence can therefore remain ambiguous for a long time: Aelthir's choices were his own, but the artifact steadily made his worst justifications feel necessary.

## Relationship to the Veil

- The Prism may remain at the Veil's metaphysical centre, which need not be the centre of the town or the point where the portal arrives.
- Damaging or removing it may threaten the plane itself, preventing “destroy the evil artifact” from being a consequence-free solution.
- Cleansing, retuning, replacing or separating its functions could become alternatives to destruction.
- The mist may be a by-product of unfiltered power, an independent force pressing against the Prism's boundaries, or the medium from which the Veil was formed. This remains unresolved.

## Foreshadowing

- Light dividing into impossible colours at old magical workings.
- Black-violet glass used as a harmless imitation in civic or religious decoration.
- Protective diagrams built from nested facets rather than circles.
- Records describing safety, stability and preservation with increasingly uncompromising language.
- Restored glyphs briefly pointing their light away from the central lantern, toward an unseen deeper source.

## Decisions to Preserve

- Who made the Prism and for what purpose.
- Whether it is sentient, instinctive or merely changes those who use it.
- Its exact location.
- Whether it can be separated from the Veil safely.
- Whether the Prism caused the present disaster or was only the tool through which it occurred.
