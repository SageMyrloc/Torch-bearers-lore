# The Vaelorn Fragments

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Almost none of Aelthir Vaelorn's original work is known to survive. Most modern knowledge of him comes from scattered quotations, copied passages and references preserved by later planar scholars.

The name **Vaelorn Fragments** is a modern convenience rather than the title of a surviving work.

It refers collectively to a small body of material attributed to [[Aelthir Vaelorn]] and preserved across later writings on artificial demiplanes.

Some fragments are passages copied from works that no longer survive. Others are only quotations or summaries written centuries later. A few survive in more than one version and disagree in wording.

Their authenticity and interpretation are therefore matters of specialist debate.

## An Obscure Source

For most historians, the fragments are little more than a curiosity from the Age of Darkness.

They are mainly of interest to the small number of scholars who study the creation and long-term stability of artificial demiplanes.

Within that field, however, the fragments contain repeated references to Vaelorn's attempts to create a place capable of enduring independently of its creator.

The later material also names the [[The Gloamheart Prism|Gloamheart Prism]] and a supposed successful creation called the [[The Veil of Aeluran|Veil of Aeluran]].

For more than four thousand years, those claims could not be verified.

The newly discovered portal may be the first physical evidence that Vaelorn's work described a real place.
