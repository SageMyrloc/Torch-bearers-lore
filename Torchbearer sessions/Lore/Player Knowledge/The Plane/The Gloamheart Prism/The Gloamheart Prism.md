# The Gloamheart Prism

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Gloamheart Prism is the powerful magical artifact Aelthir Vaelorn used as the power source when he created the Veil of Aeluran.

## Known Facts

- The Gloamheart Prism is a magical artifact of extraordinary power.
- [[Aelthir Vaelorn]] used it to create [[The Veil of Aeluran]].
- Its role as the power source for the Veil is known to the expedition before entry.

## Unknowns

- Who or what created the Prism.
- How Aelthir obtained it.
- How its power works.
- Where it is now.
- Whether it continues to sustain or influence the Veil.

## Connections

- [[Aelthir Vaelorn]] — The wizard who used the Prism.
- [[The Veil of Aeluran]] — The demiplane created through its power.
