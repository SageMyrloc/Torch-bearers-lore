# The Central Lantern

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A giant magical lantern stands in the central marketplace. Its light keeps the mist from the square and can kindle the expedition's personal lanterns.

## Known Facts

- The lantern was dark before the first recorded arrival through the portal.
- It relit when that arrival entered the marketplace.
- Its light has slowly strengthened as more people have arrived.
- Its protective reach is currently limited to the marketplace.
- [[Personal Lanterns]] can be lit from it.

## Unknowns

- Who built the lantern.
- What powers it.
- Why it responds to arrivals.
- Whether its light can be extended beyond the marketplace.

- [[Arrival and the One-Way Portal]] — The event that caused the lantern to relight.
