# The Mist - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The mist is a supernatural and exceptionally dangerous feature of the demiplane. Its origin and complete rules are not yet fixed.

## Established Truths

- The mist occupies areas beyond the reach of the lanterns' protection.
- Creatures live or travel within it.
- Characters entering it without a functioning lantern can be attacked.
- It is intended to be a major environmental threat rather than ordinary weather.

## Player Knowledge

Players begin with only the limited facts recorded in [[The Mist]] and [[Creatures in the Mist]]. Specific inhabitants, behaviours and additional consequences must be discovered during play.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Whether the mist is a creature, substance, spell, planar condition or combination of these.
- Whether it possesses intention or can be controlled.
- Whether unprotected exposure causes effects other than enabling attack.
- Whether all lantern light repels it or only magic derived from the central lantern.
- How it interacts with teleportation, divination and extradimensional spaces.

## Use in Play

Early evidence should reveal local behaviours without establishing a universal explanation. Keep descriptions consistent enough for players to make plans, while allowing different regions or threats to alter those behaviours later.
