# The Veil of Aeluran

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The mist-filled demiplane entered by the Torchbearers is known as the **Veil of Aeluran**. The wizard Aelthir Vaelorn created it using the Gloamheart Prism as its power source.

## Known Facts

- The Veil of Aeluran is a demiplane separate from [[Golarion and the Demiplane|Golarion]].
- It was created by the wizard [[Aelthir Vaelorn]].
- [[The Gloamheart Prism]] supplied the power used in its creation.
- The expedition begins with little reliable knowledge beyond its name, creator and the Prism's role as a power source.

## Unknowns

- Why Aelthir Vaelorn created the Veil.
- How the demiplane was created.
- What the Veil was originally intended to be.
- How closely its present state resembles its original form.
- Where the Gloamheart Prism is now.
