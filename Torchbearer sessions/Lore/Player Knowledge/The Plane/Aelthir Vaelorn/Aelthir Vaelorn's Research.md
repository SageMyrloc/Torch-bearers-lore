# Aelthir Vaelorn's Research

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The surviving traces of Vaelorn's work concern the creation of permanent artificial demiplanes: places able to endure without continued support from their creator.

What little survives of Aelthir Vaelorn's research concerns a problem modern scholars sometimes call **planar permanence**.

Creating an extradimensional space was not Vaelorn's principal concern. He was interested in whether such a place could endure independently: without its creator remaining present, without a continuing ritual and without relying upon a civilisation on the Material Plane to keep it stable.

## The Age of Darkness

Vaelorn worked during the Age of Darkness, after Earthfall had devastated much of Golarion and destroyed or displaced entire civilisations.

Some modern scholars believe this helps explain the repeated concern with **shelter, isolation and preservation** found in material attributed to him.

No surviving statement from Vaelorn proves what originally motivated the work, and the idea remains an interpretation rather than a known fact.

## The Problem of Permanence

Fragments attributed to Vaelorn suggest that his experiments became increasingly ambitious over time.

A temporary refuge was not enough. A place dependent upon the life of its creator was not enough. Vaelorn appears to have wanted a demiplane capable of remaining stable for generations.

Later material begins referring repeatedly to an object called the [[The Gloamheart Prism|Gloamheart Prism]].

After the Prism appears in the surviving record, Vaelorn's writings shift from largely theoretical work toward claims of practical success.

Eventually he claimed to have created a permanent demiplane.

He called it the [[The Veil of Aeluran|Veil of Aeluran]].
