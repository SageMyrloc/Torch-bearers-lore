# The Mist

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Supernatural mist fills the demiplane beyond the central lantern's protection. Entering it without a functioning lantern is exceptionally dangerous.

## Known Facts

- The mist surrounds the expedition's protected foothold.
- The mist reaches into the town wherever the central lantern's protection does not extend.
- Creatures inhabit or move through it.
- Travellers without the protection of a functioning lantern can be attacked within it.
- The expedition possesses very little reliable information about its nature.

## Unknowns

- Whether the mist is alive, controlled or merely magical.
- Whether it behaves consistently throughout the demiplane.
- What kinds of creatures dwell within it.
- Whether exposure has effects beyond the immediate danger of attack.

> [!caution] Expedition guidance
> Similarity to fog, planar vapour or a known magical effect does not establish that the mist follows familiar rules.
