# Aelthir Vaelorn

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Aelthir Vaelorn was an obscure elven wizard active during the Age of Darkness. His name survives mainly in specialist writings concerning the creation of permanent artificial demiplanes.

Very little is known about Aelthir Vaelorn.

He appears to have been an elven wizard working more than four thousand years ago, during the long Age of Darkness that followed Earthfall. He held no known position of great political importance, founded no lasting school of magic and appears only rarely in surviving histories.

His name instead survives within a particularly narrow branch of planar scholarship.

A handful of later texts concerned with **artificial demiplanes** refer to Vaelorn's experiments into creating a magical space capable of surviving permanently and independently of its creator.

Most of Vaelorn's own writings have been lost. What remains is known largely through [[The Vaelorn Fragments|scattered copies, quotations and references]] preserved by later scholars.

Those fragments eventually connect Vaelorn with two names:

**[[The Gloamheart Prism]].**

And **[[The Veil of Aeluran]].**

Until the recent discovery of the portal, neither had ever been conclusively proven to exist.
