# Personal Lanterns

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Personal lanterns carry a portion of the central lantern's magic and allow their bearers to travel into the mist for a limited time.

## Known Facts

- A personal lantern is magical.
- It must be lit from [[The Central Lantern]].
- It can emit protective light for a total of one day while active.
- Closing its shutters stops the magical light from being emitted and preserves its remaining duration.
- The lantern's limited duration makes time beyond the marketplace a finite expedition resource.

## Unresolved Practical Questions

- How precisely remaining duration can be measured.
- Whether partial shuttering changes the rate at which its magic is consumed.
- Whether damaged or separated lanterns behave differently.

These questions remain subject to observation and later campaign rulings.

## Connections

- [[The Central Lantern]] — The source from which personal lanterns are lit.
- [[The Mist]] — The danger against which their light is used.
- [[The Pathfinder Society Expedition]] — The explorers who depend on them.
