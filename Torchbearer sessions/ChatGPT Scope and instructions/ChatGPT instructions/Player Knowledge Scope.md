# Player Knowledge Scope

## Purpose

This document defines what may be treated as player knowledge when creating Torchbearers lore, handouts and sessions.

## Golarion Baseline

- The campaign uses Pathfinder Second Edition's Golarion setting and current Remastered terminology where applicable.
- Characters retain ordinary knowledge appropriate to their ancestry, culture, home region, faith, profession, training and Pathfinder Society experience.
- Broad public facts may be given freely.
- Specialist monster capabilities, obscure history, hidden religious doctrine, rare cultures and secret organisations should depend on character background or an appropriate Recall Knowledge check.
- Ancestry is the preferred PF2E term where older material might say race.

## Demiplane Baseline at Campaign Start

### Known

- The destination is a mist-filled demiplane reached through a one-way portal.
- The central marketplace is protected by a giant lantern.
- The central lantern relit with the first arrival and has strengthened as more people arrived.
- Personal lanterns are lit from it, last one active day and conserve their duration while shuttered.
- The mist is exceptionally dangerous without lantern protection.
- Creatures can attack unprotected travellers within the mist.
- Only the marketplace and inward-facing buildings are currently accessible in safety.

### Minimally Known

- The mist's nature, rules, inhabitants and origin.
- The condition and history of the wider town.

### Unknown

- The existence and function of the glyph network.
- The identity, nature and existence of the reanimated wizard antagonist.
- The origin and creator of the demiplane.
- The reasons for the portal's and lantern's behaviour.

## Authoring Rules

- Player articles must contain only facts available at the point they are issued.
- A filename, title, tag, alias or wikilink counts as disclosure.
- Do not create a player-facing article for a secret merely to say it is unknown.
- Create or revise player lore when discoveries occur so the vault becomes the expedition's accumulated record.
- Keep GM truth, theories and unresolved design decisions in GM Knowledge.
- Do not convert an inference, rumour or assistant suggestion into confirmed canon without GM approval.
