# The Barred Town

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A larger town surrounds the protected marketplace, but most of it lies beyond the central lantern's present reach and remains cut off by the mist.

## Known Facts

- The settlement extends beyond [[The Protected Marketplace]].
- The central lantern's protection does not presently cover the wider town.
- Doors and windows leading from the protected buildings into unreclaimed areas are barred.
- The expedition has not yet established the condition, extent or history of the wider settlement.

## Unknowns

- Who lived in the town.
- What caused it to be overcome by the mist.
- Why its protective light failed.
- What remains within its unreclaimed streets and buildings.

## Connections

- [[The Protected Marketplace]] — The only established safe part of the town.
- [[The Mist]] — The immediate obstacle to reclaiming the wider settlement.
- [[Personal Lanterns]] — The means by which explorers can leave the protected square.
