# The Protected Marketplace

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The central marketplace and the buildings facing into it form the expedition's only established safe foothold in the town.

## Known Facts

- [[The Central Lantern]] stands within the marketplace.
- Its light currently protects the square from [[The Mist]].
- Arrivals through the portal enter here.
- The buildings facing inward onto the marketplace are accessible from the protected area.
- Doors and windows leading away from the square have been barred against the mist.

## Current Importance

The marketplace serves as the expedition's point of arrival, shelter and staging area. Leaving its light requires [[Personal Lanterns]] and exposes explorers to the demiplane's poorly understood dangers.

## Connections

- [[The Barred Town]] — The unreclaimed settlement surrounding the marketplace.
- [[The Central Lantern]] — The source of the marketplace's protection.
- [[The Pathfinder Society Expedition]] — The people using the marketplace as their foothold.
