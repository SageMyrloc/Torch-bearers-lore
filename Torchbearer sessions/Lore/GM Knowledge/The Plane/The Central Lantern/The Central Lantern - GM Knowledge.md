# The Central Lantern - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The central lantern is the present source of the town's safe light. The glyph network distributes its protection but does not create that power.

## Established Truths

- The lantern stands in the central marketplace.
- It relit when the first arrival crossed the portal.
- It has strengthened as more people have arrived.
- Its current unaided protection is limited to the marketplace.
- It can kindle personal lanterns.
- [[The Glyph Network]] once carried its protective power across the town.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Who constructed it and the glyph network.
- What source of power the lantern uses.
- Why people arriving through the portal strengthen it.
- Whether that strengthening has a limit, cost or hidden consequence.
- Whether the lantern was extinguished, deliberately shut down or starved of power before the first arrival.

## Use in Play

The lantern should provide visible campaign progress as glyphs are restored and safe territory expands. Early clues may establish its relationship to the network without revealing its origin.
