# The Town at Campaign Start - GM Knowledge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Only the central marketplace and its inward-facing buildings are accessible in safety. The rest of the town remains behind barricades and within the mist.

## Established Truths

- The portal delivers arrivals into the central marketplace.
- The central lantern currently protects only that square.
- Buildings facing inward are accessible from the safe area.
- Doors and windows leading farther into town are barred against the mist.
- Restoring the glyph network will progressively reclaim the town.

## Unresolved Decisions

- The town's name, age, builders and original population.
- Whether anyone still survives beyond the marketplace.
- Who erected the current barricades and when.
- How the glyph network maps onto districts or major locations.
- What caused the network and the wider town to fail.

## Use in Play

The safe area should expand as a direct consequence of expeditions. Newly protected locations can provide services, resources, clues, complications and visible signs of settlement growth.
