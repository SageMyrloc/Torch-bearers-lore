# The Reanimated Wizard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The campaign's current principal antagonist is a worm-that-walks wizard formed from the reanimated corpse of Aelthir Vaelorn. Its identity and nature are intended as later revelations.

## Established Truths

- The antagonist is presently conceived as a worm that walks and a wizard.
- It was formed from the reanimated corpse of the wizard who created the Veil.
- Players do not know this at campaign start.
- The revelation should occur later in the campaign rather than during the opening sessions.

## Provisional Working Direction

> [!warning] Not yet approved canon

- The creature retains fragments of Aelthir's technical memory but may not contain his soul or complete personality.
- It experiences the glyph network as a severed magical nervous system. Each restored glyph allows it to sense farther into the town.
- It initially benefits from the Torchbearers' repairs but cannot simply prevent them, because the same restored light restricts the creatures and mist it relies upon.
- Its behaviour therefore appears contradictory: it may obstruct one expedition, protect another, or leave materials that help restore a glyph.
- Its eventual aim could be to reach the Gloamheart Prism, complete Aelthir's last unfinished command, or take control of the restored network.

## Unresolved Decisions

- Whether the creature is Aelthir, carries only his memories, or merely uses his corpse.
- Who or what reanimated the body.
- Whether the Gloamheart Prism caused, enabled or merely survived the reanimation.
- Its final objective and what it believes will happen if it succeeds.
- Why it does not confront the Torchbearers directly.
- Whether “worm that walks” will use an official PF2E creature framework, a custom creature, or a unique homebrew interpretation.

## Reveal Discipline

- Do not name the creature type or connect it to Aelthir in player-facing material.
- Foreshadow through consequences, traces and contradictory evidence rather than direct exposition.
- Let discoveries establish that something understands Aelthir's systems before revealing what that something is.
- Avoid confirming whether Aelthir's soul survives until that distinction matters to a player decision.
