# Creatures in the Mist

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The expedition knows that hostile creatures can attack those who enter the mist without lantern protection. It does not yet possess a reliable catalogue of those creatures.

## Known Facts

- At least some creatures can survive or travel within [[The Mist]].
- Unprotected travellers are vulnerable to attack.
- No particular ancestry, species or type of creature should be assumed before it is observed and identified.

## Beliefs and Accounts

> [!question] Uncertain knowledge
> Descriptions given before direct examination may be incomplete, mistaken or shaped by poor visibility. Individual encounters should be recorded separately once the expedition has reliable evidence.

## Connections

- [[The Mist]] — The creatures' known environment.
- [[Personal Lanterns]] — The expedition's protection when entering that environment.
