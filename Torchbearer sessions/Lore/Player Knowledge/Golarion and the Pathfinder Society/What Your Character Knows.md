# What Your Character Knows

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Torchbearers retain the ordinary knowledge they would possess on Golarion. Before entering the Veil, the expedition was briefed on the small amount of surviving scholarship connected to its creator.

## Knowledge of Golarion

A character may know the broad, public facts appropriate to their upbringing, ancestry, faith, profession, training and time with the Pathfinder Society. This can include common knowledge about:

- Golarion's peoples, nations and cultures.
- Widely worshipped gods and their public teachings.
- Well-known creatures, hazards and magical traditions.
- The public purpose and practices of the Pathfinder Society.

Specialist facts, obscure history, exact monster abilities, secret religious teachings and unfamiliar cultures may require Recall Knowledge or an appropriate Lore skill during play.

## Knowledge of the Veil

The expedition knows that its destination is a demiplane called the **Veil of Aeluran**, created by an obscure elven wizard named [[Aelthir Vaelorn]] using [[The Gloamheart Prism]] as a power source.

Aelthir worked during the Age of Darkness and is known mainly to specialists who study the creation of permanent artificial demiplanes. Most surviving information about him comes from scattered copies, quotations and later references known collectively as [[The Vaelorn Fragments]].

The fragments show that Aelthir was attempting to create a demiplane capable of enduring independently of its creator. Some modern scholars connect his interest in shelter and preservation with the devastation that followed Earthfall, but the precise reason he created the Veil remains unknown.

Characters may recognise similarities between phenomena inside the Veil and things known on Golarion, but resemblance is not proof that those phenomena follow the same rules here.

The player-facing articles in this archive represent the expedition's currently reliable shared knowledge. They can expand as the Torchbearers make discoveries.
